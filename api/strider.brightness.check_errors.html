check_errors¶

strider.brightness.check_errors(days, band, flux, error) → ErrorCheck[source]¶

How far a light curve’s errors understate its scatter, from the light curve alone.

In each band, each row less the quadratic through its SCALE_NEAR neighbours on each side in time (least squares) is divided by that residual’s noise from the errors, kept where the neighbours’ summed flux is below SCALE_FAINT in S/N, as a bright light curve’s curvature is not noise. With s^2 the mean squared residual and se its standard error were the errors right (Gaussian, from the residuals’ exact correlations), the scale is sqrt(max(1, s^2 - SCALE_Z se)). Too few independent residuals (se above SCALE_SE, as for a short light curve, whose few residuals sit at its bright, curved part) measure nothing, and the scale is 1. Known limitation: the faint test reads the flux as given, so constant host light in a band, which the term removes, can leave no faint rows to measure.

Parameters:

  • days (array_like) – The rows, as BrightnessTerm.score takes them; rows without a finite flux and a positive error are left out.

  • band (array_like) – The rows, as BrightnessTerm.score takes them; rows without a finite flux and a positive error are left out.

  • flux (array_like) – The rows, as BrightnessTerm.score takes them; rows without a finite flux and a positive error are left out.

  • error (array_like) – The rows, as BrightnessTerm.score takes them; rows without a finite flux and a positive error are left out.
