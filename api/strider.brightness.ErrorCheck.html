ErrorCheck¶

class strider.brightness.ErrorCheck(scale: float, scatter: float, scatter_squared_se: float, points: int)[source]¶

Bases: NamedTuple

How a light curve’s scatter compares with its errors (check_errors).

Parameters:

  • scale (float) – The factor to multiply the errors by: sqrt(max(1, s^2 - 2 se)), or 1 if the scatter was not measured.

  • scatter (float) – s, the scatter of the faint points about their neighbours over what the errors allow; NaN without points.

  • scatter_squared_se (float) – se, the standard error of s^2 were the errors right; NaN without points.

  • points (int) – How many faint points measured it.
